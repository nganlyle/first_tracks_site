One of Big White's original, cherished buildings

This location is one of the best on the mountain — just a 4-minute walk to the hill, with ski-out access to three express lifts and the Village a short stroll away. It's a taste of simpler times, with sweeping views and an authentic mountain-community feel.

Large windows on two sides fill the corner unit with light and warm southwest exposure. After a day on the slopes, unwind in your own private indoor sauna — added in 2024 — then curl up by the electric fireplace. Watch the Saturday night fireworks from the couch, or head down to the lobby to mingle with other guests.

The kitchen is fully equipped, including a dishwasher, a regular coffee maker with a reusable filter, and a Nespresso machine (bring your own Original Line capsules). Free on-site parking is available for one vehicle, first come first served — let us know ahead of time if you'll need a second spot.
